I read Ibn Khaldun’s account of the Zā’irja, a medieval **“decision-making” device**. I can't accept most of the claims, but one part was making sense for me: a set of terms/words can produce questions I would not ask on my own.

I vibe coded a small web app from this idea. The following app combines a problem, a way to act, a constraint, a new point of view, and most importantly human goal.

**Explore the project: [https://hasantayyar.net/assets/possibility-engine/](https://hasantayyar.net/assets/possibility-engine/)**

I was reading[ “Scrambling T-R-U-T-H Rotating Letters as a Material Form of Thought” by David Link](https://web.archive.org/web/20200125104105/http://www.alpha60.de/research/scrambling_truth/DavidLink_ScramblingTruth2010_100dpi.pdf).

I stumbled upon that article while reading about Ramon Llull’s combinatorial machines.

From there jumped to the __Zā’irja__, another paper device/machine.
Historians accept that __Llull__ was heavily and directly **inspired** by the Ibn Khaldun's Zā’irja**.
Ramon Llull was living in Majorca and learned Arabic. He was exposed to Islamic science, astrology, and mechanics.

Zā’irja, developed by Ibn Khaldun, is also a paper machine like Llull's machine.
It’s a medieval **“decision-making” device**. A mechanical **paper computational machine** developed in the Islamic world and analyzed by historian ***Ibn Khaldun*** in his book the *Muqaddimah*.

Ibn Khaldun completed the first draft of his work/book ***Muqaddimah*** (*The Introduction*) in **1377 CE**.

He wrote it while he he was in self-imposed isolation at the castle of __Qal'at Ibn Salama__, nowadays Algeria. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muqaddimah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muqaddimah)

It operates like an analog computer or a systematic ideation tool designed to generate answers to complex questions.

How It Worked

1. **Input:** You input a clear question written in Arabic.
2. **Breakdown:** The letters of the question are converted into numbers using traditional letter-number systems (Abjad numerology).
3. **Processing:** The data is run through dynamic, concentric circles containing astronomical, cosmological, and mathematical rules.
4. **Output:** The machine rearranges the elements to produce a poetic or insightful answer.
