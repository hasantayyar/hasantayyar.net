"[Chromatic Drift](https://hasantayyar.net/assets/chromatic-drift-p5-visual-work/)" is a visual work to to capture the gradual color transitions and the smooth, cloud-like movement of the organic natural pastel brush strokes.

See the project: [https://hasantayyar.net/assets/chromatic-drift-p5-visual-work/](https://hasantayyar.net/assets/chromatic-drift-p5-visual-work/)

I used p5.js and p5.brush libraries to make this. Inspired from this work [editor.p5js.org/acamposuribe/sketches/gSQP6hHbi](https://editor.p5js.org/acamposuribe/sketches/gSQP6hHbi)
