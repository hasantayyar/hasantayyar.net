A mind map of the foundational concepts of Karate-Do, integrating technique and spirit, presented:

```
Foundational Concepts of Karate-Do: Technique and Spirit
├── 1. Karate-Do: Way of Life and Self-Actualisation
│   ├── Definition: Comprehensive way of life integrating body, mind, and spirit [prev. turn]
│   │   └── "Do" signifies a "way" or "path" for self-actualisation and awakening consciousness [prev. turn, 35]
│   ├── Distinction from Karate-jutsu: Focuses on developing the whole person through traditional art, not just combat techniques [prev. turn]
│   ├── Highest Perfection: Aims for physical, spiritual, and mental perfection [prev. turn, 310]
│   ├── Samurai Influence: Ethical and moral foundation from samurai values, including Zen and Bushido [prev. turn, 309, 318, 320]
│   └── Continuous Study: Emphasizes continuous study over a lifetime, personal growth, and self-discovery [prev. turn, 24, 310]
│
├── 2. The Unity of Mind, Body, and Spirit (Shin Gi Tai)
│   ├── Concept: Harmony of body and mind [prev. turn, 619]
│   ├── Physical (Tai/Gi)
│   │   ├── Developing strength, stamina, physical well-being [prev. turn, 308]
│   │   ├── Rigorous techniques, stances, blocks, punches, kicks [prev. turn, 308]
│   │   ├── Understanding biomechanics, body rotation, power generation [prev. turn, 215, 251, 270]
│   │   └── "Tai no Shinshuku": Refers to body expansion and contraction
│   ├── Mental (Shin)
│   │   ├── Cultivates mental alertness, concentration, self-awareness, agility over brute strength [prev. turn, 36, 477]
│   │   ├── Strategies, decision-making, controlling emotions (fear, insecurity) [prev. turn, 388, 575]
│   │   └── "Shingi Ittai": Mind and technique are one
│   └── Spiritual (Shin)
│       ├── Character development, ethical conduct, humility, respect, sincerity [prev. turn, 24, 320, 488, 490, 605, 606]
│       ├── Rooted in Zen philosophy, self-discovery, "forgetting the small self" [prev. turn, 305, 309, 313, 337]
│       └── Consciousness as an energy field vibrating at different frequencies
│
├── 3. Kime (Focus and Decision)
│   ├── Definition: Complete focus of an execution of a technique from beginning to end, resulting in the perfect finish [prev. turn, 282, 480, 596]
│   ├── Encapsulation of Energies: Brings together all degrees of energies at any particular moment in space and time [prev. turn, 40, 41, 141]
│   ├── Physical Kime
│   │   ├── Applies to the entire path of a technique [prev. turn, 282]
│   │   ├── Strong contraction of all body muscles (hips outwards to limbs) synchronized with breathing [prev. turn, 10, 480]
│   │   └── Requires correct outward and internal form, body alignment, timing, Intra-Abdominal Pressure (IAP), breathing
│   │       └── Encapsulates timed body expansion and contraction
│   ├── Mental Kime
│   │   ├── Focusing on external and internal happenings
│   │   ├── External: Control of mind to place attacking limb accurately against a moving target
│   │   ├── Internal: Coordinating neuromuscular pathways, breath, coordination
│   │   ├── Substantive knowledge and control of conscious and subconscious mind [prev. turn, 72]
│   │   └── Uses mental strategies like Maai, Hyoshi, Kuzushi, Yomi
│   └── Spiritual Kime
│       ├── Wanting to hit the target, emotional focus, will to drive into the target
│       ├── Cosmic nature, commitment to art and Senseis, love for students
│       ├── Bringing all levels of consciousness/energy into present moment awareness
│       ├── Ultimate aim of the Martial Artist, separates adepts from novices
│       └── "Yomi": A concept that lies between Mental and Spiritual Kime
│
├── 4. Stances (Dachi)
│   ├── Foundation: Bedrock of karate practice [prev. turn, 9, 519]
│   ├── Purpose: Stable base for techniques, controlling body position and distance, dynamic movement [prev. turn, 9, 235, 519]
│   ├── Categories: Natural (*shizen tai*), fundamental (*kihon*), sparring (*kumite*) [prev. turn, 520, 521, 522, 524]
│   ├── Key Aspects
│   │   ├── Proper weight distribution, foot position, leg tension, hip position [prev. turn, 235]
│   │   ├── Knee always bends in direction of foot points to prevent injury [prev. turn]
│   │   ├── Dynamic stances are adaptable, not rigidly fixed [prev. turn, 234]
│   │   ├── Stances use dynamic tension to transition body weight for techniques
│   │   └── Inside and outside tension are critical for movement between stances
│   ├── Examples: *Zenkutsu Dachi* (front stance), *Kiba Dachi* (horse stance), *Kokutsu Dachi* (back stance), *Sanchin Dachi* (hourglass stance), *Heiko Dachi* (parallel stance), *Uchi Hachiji Dachi* (inside natural stance), *Moto Dachi* (neutral stance), *Shiko Dachi* (sumo stance), *Neko Ashi Dachi* (cat stance), *Fudo Dachi* (rooted stance) [prev. turn, 118-123, 231, 232, 233, 342, 459, 460, 527]
│   └── Spirit: Represent ideal-world postures for correct body shapes and technique-position connection [prev. turn]
│
├── 5. Breathing (Kokyu) and Hara/Tanden
│   ├── Importance: Key to coordination, vital for power generation and movement control [prev. turn, 10, 273, 278, 328, 481, 514]
│   ├── Technique
│   │   ├── Body expansion and contraction inextricably linked to timing [prev. turn, 273, 278]
│   │   ├── Power from *seika tanden* (lower abdomen), *hara* (centre of gravity) [prev. turn, 23, 330, 394, 395, 479, 483]
│   │   ├── *Ibuki* breathing: Tensing abdominal area, forceful exhalation, synchronized with timing [prev. turn, 331, 333, 368, 370, 371, 394]
│   │   ├── Conscious, deep, natural breathing harmonises *Qi* (life force) and massages internal organs [prev. turn, 23, 629, 394]
│   │   ├── Inhalation used to relax/body contraction; exhalation used for body expansion, tensing muscles, IAP
│   │   ├── Pulse breathing: Multiple techniques in single exhalation by using diaphragm and chest expansion/contraction
│   │   └── IAP can be achieved on either inward or outward breaths
│   └── Spirit
│       ├── Breath control, strengthening abdominal area, spiritual unification recognized in Zen, Tao, Yoga [prev. turn, 23, 27, 28, 74, 75, 76, 394]
│       ├── Coordination of breathing links to mind and spirit, contributing to Kime [prev. turn, 57, 283]
│       ├── Zen exhalation involves squeezing lower abdomen, leaning forward
│       ├── Taoist breathing (*Fan Hu Xi*): Contracts abdomen on inhale, expands on exhale; reverse of Buddhist breathing; more tense and efficient for expanding *Wei Qi*
│       └── Buddhist breathing: Expand abdomen on inhale, contract on exhale
│           └── Visualization techniques used to direct *Chi* (*Ki*) to *Tantem* (DanTian) and other energy reservoirs (*Huiyin*, *Yuzhen*)
│
├── 6. Kata (Formal Exercises) and Bunkai (Applications)
│   ├── Definition: Ancient sets of movements, choreographed sequences of techniques, loaded with hidden self-defense techniques [prev. turn, 203, 346, 461]
│   ├── Essence of Training: Considered the essence of karate training [prev. turn, 346, 556]
│   ├── Technique
│   │   ├── Blocking, evading, counter-attacking, striking, punching, kicking [prev. turn, 203, 359]
│   │   ├── Self-practice and physical conditioning: strength, agility, coordination, control [prev. turn, 203, 308]
│   │   ├── Movements performed with varied timing and in many directions [prev. turn]
│   │   ├── Promotes neuro-muscular networks and muscle memory [prev. turn, 256, 68]
│   │   ├── *Bunkai* (expanded application) and *Bunkai Oyo* (practical interpretation): deciphering hidden meanings and self-defense applications [prev. turn, 23, 561, 609, 612]
│   │   ├── Kata often contain encoded *Kyusho* (sensitive points) and *Tuite* (joint leverage) techniques [prev. turn, 611, 613, 618, 620]
│   │   ├── No unnecessary movements; each movement is economical
│   │   ├── Stances in Kata are not ballet, they are suited to the exercise
│   │   └── *Embusen* (movement pattern) should not be rigidly adhered to, adapt as required by situation
│   └── Spirit
│       ├── Improves mind and body, continuity between mental and physical actions [prev. turn, 205, 555]
│       ├── Vehicle for character development, fostering calm mind, harmony [prev. turn, 313, 351, 488]
│       ├── Names often carry philosophical meanings (e.g., *Heian* means peace, *Taikyoku* means "wide view") [prev. turn, 348, 358]
│       ├── Proper attitude uncovers deeper meanings, connecting modern practice to traditional wisdom [prev. turn, 349]
│       ├── *Ren Ma* ("constant polishing"): Unceasing, repetitive daily practice of basics
│       ├── *Shuchu Ryoku*: Spiritual energy concentrated on kata, leading to beautiful, strong, powerful performance
│       ├── *Mushin* ("no mind"): Kata ingrained in subconscious, automatic response without conscious thought
│       ├── Kata as "moving Zen"
│       ├── *Zanshin* (remaining mind) and *Isshin* (one mind): Balance between calmness and intensity
│       └── *Chion* (sound knowledge) is foundation for performing kata properly
│
├── 7. Kumite (Fighting) and Self-Defense
│   ├── Purpose: Training to improve techniques against an opponent, preparing for real situations [prev. turn, 11, 308, 309, 562]
│   ├── Self-Defense Foundation: Karate's core purpose is self-defense [prev. turn, 1, 3, 20, 451, 466, 529]
│   ├── Technique
│   │   ├── Basic, semi-free, and free sparring [prev. turn, 3]
│   │   ├── Develops techniques, speed, timing, distance (*maai*), practical functionality [prev. turn, 10, 228, 462]
│   │   ├── Offensive and counter-offensive strategies [prev. turn, 3]
│   │   ├── Blocks (*uke*): Deflection and setting up counter-attacks [prev. turn, 126, 244, 531]
│   │   ├── Techniques: *tsuki* (thrusting), *keri* (kicking), *uchi* (striking) [prev. turn, 223]
│   │   ├── Effective techniques require vigorous application, speed, power, controlled to prevent injury [prev. turn, 10, 257]
│   │   └── *Sensation, Stabilisation, Explosion (SSE)* sequence for powerful blocks
│   └── Spirit
│       ├── "Real competition is with the self" [prev. turn, 305, 310, 320, 595]
│       ├── Non-violence frame of mind, techniques as last resort [prev. turn, 89, 96, 344]
│       ├── Calmness, awareness, controlled mind [prev. turn, 344, 487, 575]
│       ├── *Yomi* (reading opponent's intention) and *Mushin* (mind no mind) for combat [prev. turn, 73, 126, 128, 131, 133, 134, 135, 136, 137, 193, 194]
│       └── *Toh Kon* (Fighting Spirit)
│
├── 8. Etiquette and Character Development
│   ├── Integral to Karate-Do: Cultivates self-awareness and sensitivity to others [prev. turn, 605, 606]
│   ├── Formality and Courtesy: How uniforms are worn, moving, speaking to seniors, bowing (*rei*) [prev. turn, 319, 456, 493, 505, 605, 607]
│   │   ├── Bowing performed at beginning and end of kata [prev. turn, 205]
│   │   └── Signifies respect, self-control, forgetting the "small self" and ego [prev. turn, 337, 519]
│   ├── Core Principles: Respect, love, and obedience [prev. turn, 319, 605]
│   └── Character: Encourages commendable character, sincerity, effort, etiquette, self-control [prev. turn, 24, 488, 490, 580]
│       ├── Humility, prevents destructive use of skills, promotes ethical behavior [prev. turn, 344, 424]
│       ├── *Omoiyari* (thoughtfulness, sincere thoughtfulness)
│       ├── *Ko Gaku Shin* (keep mind open to learn)
│       ├── *Fu Gen Jikko* (let actions speak for you)
│       ├── *Mei Kyo Shi Sui* (bright mirror calmly reflects the world, but is not changed by it)
│       ├── *Ichi Nichi Issho* (one day – one lifetime)
│       ├── *Setsu Do Motsu* (be strong, know when to bend)
│       ├── *Gassho* (be grateful for each moment)
│       ├── *Heijo shin* (everyday mind)
│       ├── *Wabi* (disciplined restraint to create simple and elegant art)
│       └── White uniform symbolizes purity, beginner's mind (*Sho Shin*)
│
├── 9. Balance (Kuzushi) and Movement (Unsoku/Tai Sabaki)
│   ├── Balance (Kuzushi)
│   │   ├── Refers to breaking an opponent's balance by moving their center of gravity outside their base of support [prev. turn, 28, 121, 122, 123, 97]
│   │   ├── Exploiting weak points in stance or moving them where they are already weak [prev. turn]
│   │   ├── Physical balance is a reflexive, automatic process [prev. turn, 116, 246]
│   │   ├── *Static Kuzushi*: Applies to unbalancing a static opponent
│   │   ├── *Dynamic Kuzushi*: Unbalancing an opponent in a Zenkutsu dashi, breaking axis of stance
│   │   ├── *Subtle Kuzushi*: Influencing opponent through mirror neurons with hidden hip/knee/shoulder movements
│   │   └── *Mental Kuzuchi*: Using feints or presenting targets to create asymmetry
│   └── Movement (Unsoku/Tai Sabaki)
│       ├── Footwork (*unsoku*) and body movements (*tai sabaki*) fundamental to maintaining/limiting opponent's range, creating momentum, avoiding attacks [prev. turn, 28, 102, 122]
│       ├── Techniques can be static or dynamic [prev. turn]
│       ├── Various steps: *Ayumi Ashi* (frontward walking), *Hiki Ashi* (backward walking), *Yori Ashi* (sliding), *Tsugi Ashi* (shuffle), *Okuri Ashi* (single/double step), *Mawari Ashi* (pivoting on front foot), *Ushiro Mawari Ashi* (pivoting on back foot), *Kae Ashi* (switching legs) [prev. turn, 28, 104-111]
│       ├── *Ukimi*: Suspension and floating of legs underneath body center, body center controls legs, feeling light [prev. turn, 85, 92, 102]
│       ├── *Zui Ban*: Shifting for hidden/camouflaged movement, sequential movement with unnoticeable time lapse [prev. turn, 93, 103]
│       └── Importance of *Hyoshi* (cadence/rhythm) for winning strategy [prev. turn, 28, 112, 113, 114, 115]
│           ├── *A Taru Hyoshi* (engage opponent), *Ma No Hyoshi* (break off/retreat), *Somuku Hyoshi* (use opposite rhythms)
│           ├── *Ku No Hyoshi* (blended Hyoshi) confuses opponent
│           └── Directly forged in breathing
│
└── 10. Origins and Philosophical Influences
    ├── Okinawan Origins: *Te* (hands) influenced by Chinese boxing (*chuan-fa* or *gongfu*) into styles like Shuri-te, Naha-te [prev. turn, 418, 419, 421, 603]

    ├── Evolution: Early masters like Funakoshi Gichin and Chojun Miyagi emphasized character training and self-improvement beyond combat [prev. turn, 24, 36, 305, 309, 605, 619]
    ├── Zen Influence: Samurai value system, *Bushido*, ethical framework [prev. turn, 309, 317, 318, 320, 606]
    │   ├── Zen teaches direct, personal experience and practice of *zazen* (seated meditation)
    │   └── *Moving Zen*: Total involvement and absorption in present moment
    ├── Perennial Philosophy/Great Chain of Being: Reality composed of continuous dimensions from dense/least conscious (matter) to subtle/most conscious (spirit/God-head) [prev. turn, 42, 43, 44, 142, 186]
    │   └── Widely accepted across cultures and ages
    │   └── Hinduism, Buddhism, Taoism all describe these energy levels/realms
    ├── Chakras/Energy Vortexes: Seven main vortexes (Muladhara, Svadhistara, Manipura, Anahata, Vishuddha, Ajna, Sahasrara) and three main channels (Sushumna, Ida, Pingala)
    │   ├── Connected to different energy bodies/realms
    │   └── *Kundalini*: Powerful energy residing dormant at root chakra, rises through Sushumna, awakening higher bodies
    └── Mantras & Kototamas: Sounds used in meditation to move up the ladder to subtler energies
        ├── *Pranayama* (breath control): Developed by Hindu Sages to control and move energy through chakras
        └── Mantras (Sanskrit sounds) and Kototamas (Japanese sacred sounds of the soul)
```
